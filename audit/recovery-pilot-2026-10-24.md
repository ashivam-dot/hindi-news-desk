# Matlabnama recovery pilot

Keep the current 20–25-second news format, voice, visual style and cadence steady for ten mature uploads before
another format decision. The 24 October writer repair changes bounded draft repair and fallback routing, not facts,
licences or quality thresholds. `compare.ENABLED` stays false while the source licensing is unresolved.

Newly prepared Shorts carry `scene.production_version = news-shorts-2026-10-24` and the producer's `GITHUB_SHA`.
Publication and performance records retain these fields; publishing an old artifact does not label it as new.
The label records a production cohort, not a claimed duration or quality pass. Inspect the rendered duration.

1. Record each attempt, exact failing stage, production minutes and upload ID/privacy. A green run is not a release.
2. Review the opening, middle and payoff, listen to the narration, and check captions and each named person/prop.
3. Prioritize fresh, significant, showable news. A follow-up requires a verified new development; separate `-update`
   stories from fresh news and keep explainers, incidents and obituaries distinct in comparisons.
4. Collect direct observations at 24–30 and 48–54 hours; record lagged Analytics and its covered dates separately.
   Do not relabel `views_24h` interpolation/backcasts as measurements. Missing engaged reach, retention and conversion
   stay unknown.
5. Review after the tenth pilot upload has reached 54 hours. Compare measured reach, retention, shares and
   subscribers per 1,000 engaged views where available; record topics, slot, story age and actual runtime as confounders.
   This is an exploratory pilot, not a causal experiment. Repeat a promising finding before changing the format.

Hosted provider code without configured keys and workflow environment wiring is not an operational fallback.
Use the existing local models within the bounded attempts; never relax sourcing or picture checks to fill a quota.
